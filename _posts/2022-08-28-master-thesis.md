In June 2022 I finished my master degree at the [Institute of Informatics](https://www.mn.uio.no/ifi/english/) at the University of Oslo. My master thesis, titled _Virtual LiDAR error models in point cloud compression_ is now available on the [UiO Research Archive](http://urn.nb.no/URN:NBN:no-98124). 

I enjoyed working in a different area than my day job and I learned a great deal about LiDAR technology, sensor simulation, machine learning and compression.
